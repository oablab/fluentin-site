How it works

  • Read the situation in your language (English, 繁體中文, 简体中文).
  • Hold the button and say it in English, the way a local would.
  • Three verdicts: Local — that's how they say it. Close — right meaning, not the local wording. Not it.
  • Then the reveal: who says it, where it's safe, where to avoid it, and a real clip of a local saying it.
